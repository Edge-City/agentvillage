##### Edge City convenes people working at the frontiers of tech, science, and society in popup villages across the globe.

##### Edge City is a society incubator: an IRL lab of experimentation for new ideas, tech, culture, and organizations, all dedicated to accelerating human flourishing.

##### ‍[Read our roadmap](https://www.edgecity.live/roadmap) for more details.

Our villages last 1-2 months and are perfect environments to live, learn, and experiment with frontier projects. Participants comefrom diverse fields, including AI, BioTech & Longevity, Crypto, Philosophy, Culture, Hard Tech, Governance, and more. Edge City is part of and contributes to the [Zuzalu](https://www.palladiummag.com/2023/10/06/why-i-built-zuzalu/) ecosystem.

‍

**We believe the best opportunity for positive impact comes from multidisciplinary collaboration. Our gatherings are perfectly designed for this,** with longer durations and structured community design that foster deep connection and partnership between attendees. Edge City is an ideal setting for innovators to work alongside their peers, tackling major problems focused on enhancing societal prosperity and individual fulfillment.

‍

**Why create spaces for this type of experimentation?** 

‍

Because he systems that shape our time—institutions, norms, governance structures—were made for a prior world. Ones that worked in the past are now rigid and slow-moving, unable to evolve to meet the opportunities and challenges of the current moment.

‍

By creating “[**micro-exits**](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/08/21/plurality.html#:~:text=There%20is%20still%20some%20choice%20and%20exit%20in%20Audrey%27s%20vision%2C%20but%20there%20is%20a%20much%20tighter%20feedback%20loop%20by%20which%20the%20improvements%20created%20by%20micro%2Dexits%20get%20merged%20back%20into%20%22mainline%22%20societal%20infrastructure.),” we create space to experiment with new systems, technologies, and practices. What works in these experimental environments can then be shared with broader society, shaping a brighter future for everyone.

‍

> _"Edge City did an excellent job integrating diverse participants from all kinds of backgrounds: technical and non-technical, crypto, biotech and culture, young and old. I learned a lot from my time there” — Vitalik Buterin_

#### Four Key Design Principles

Our popup villages and gatherings are shaped by four key design principles: we focus on **health, multidisciplinarity, a culture of co-creation, and making sure that the events are family-friendly and multigenerational (we make it easy for folks with families to bring their kids).**

**‍**

**‍**These aspects are critical because we know that if we actually want to experiment with building a better future, we need people to be able to live a holistic experience that combines the full variety of their lives. We’ve seen how a focus on these aspects creates a healthier, more productive lifestyle for the time that they are at Edge City. 

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65b2cb5abdecf7cd7747e170/67437b13238f4d4330510f83_AD_4nXcJHhLATQFN2OB1SxZp2EQAx3tmq6eok8KsxP3QC1HLz-ehd6rMq6VZGUFeY6AEYZ2WQ5BKn4JTcc11xPnB_ceuYQ_L4ZNGEPgqYNvQLPIMWv0eyPsJGkWgc_aEuVolphh25_WJFA.avif)

The villages take place around the world. **We purposefully bring people to brand new environments that help them see beyond the ingrained patterns of thought and activity of their default lives.** It also allows us to prioritize the diversity of participants by being able to invite people who may have visa issues in accessing traditional innovation hubs.

‍

Our attendees are all **curious, kind, and high-agency** — working on something towards a brighter future.

## The Impact\

Edge City creates impact in three key areas:

‍

1.  **Accelerate Resident Impact**\
    We fill our villages with curious, kind, ambitious people who give each other the courage, education, and inspiration to apply their talents to do the most impactful work of their lives. Through [grants, fellowships, and residencies](https://www.edgecity.live/#grants), we support builders with bold ideas.**‍**
2.  **Incubate Projects**\
    We foster the development of groundbreaking technologies and [experiments](https://www.edgecity.live/#experiments). Our community is keen to build and test out prototypes and give feedback, which is critical to helping beat the cold start problem for novel ideas. **‍**
3.  **Advance Governance and Community Models**\
    We will demonstrate new models for community living. Popup villages are real-life laboratories for new decision-making and organizational models, allowing us to try innovative governance and community designs.
