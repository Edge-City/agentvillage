## What to expect

Our Road to Edge hosts, **Ashu** and **Morello**, will be leading you and a small group by bus through 6 farms and communities across South India: 1,000km, 10 days, starting in Bangalore and ending in Goa right in time for week one of [Edge City India](https://www.edgecity.live/india26). Along the way:

-   Visit farms worked by the same families for 6–7 generations

-   Walk 200-year-old indigenous farms still in daily use

-   Get hands-on with permaculture, natural building, and traditional crafts

-   Surf, make chocolate, join a cacao ceremony, kayak, practice chi gong, and drop into village life

-   Take part in yoga sessions and indigenous wisdom circles

You’ll get to explore the places and learn from the people who have been figuring out regenerative life for decades, in some cases for generations: eco-villages, indigenous farms, and permaculture projects that have outlasted every trend cycle.

In Ashu's words: **"If you want to build communities for the future, you need to learn from communities that have thrived from the past to now."**

## Tentative 10-Day Plan

**Day 1 — Bangalore → Mysore\
**The caravan begins with an opening circle, introductions, and the drive to Mysore. There’s no agenda beyond getting to know who you’re traveling with.

**Day 2 — Mysore\
**The day includes morning yoga, a look at Mysore’s living relationship with modern yoga, and an evening kirtan.

**Day 3 — Mysore → Coorg\
**This is a coffee estate immersion, covering growing, harvesting, processing, and tasting, along with the real economics behind one cup.

**Day 4 — Coorg\
**We visit a regenerative land collective and hear from the founders about land acquisition and collective ownership models, then spend time at a waterfall and in the forest.

**Days 5–7 — Varanashi Farm\
**This is the heart of the caravan: an organic farm tour, food forests and water systems, sunrise kayaking, soil biology, hands-on paddy farming, a bean-to-bar chocolate workshop, water harvesting, and permaculture design. It closes with a reflection dinner on the farm.

**Day 8 — Varanashi → Udupi (Coast)\
**We spend the day in coastal India, visiting a traditional fishing harbour and learning about coastal livelihoods, mangrove ecosystems, and local coastal food.

**Day 9 — Udupi → Gokarna\
**We explore Gokarna’s old town and temple culture, spend time at the beach, and hold our closing circle. It’s the last night together as a full caravan.

**Day 10 — Gokarna → Edge City, Goa\
**The caravan makes its final drive north to the pop-up village, arriving at Edge City for a group photo and handover.

Similar to how we run programming during our villages, the roadtrip has an element of co-creation embedded in it. The best sessions on trips like this often come from participants themselves. So if you’ve got a workshop, a skill, or an offering that fits the group, bring it.

_Itinerary is tentative and subject to change based on access, weather, and season._

### About the Indian Eco Village Network

Founded by **Ashu**, the Indian Eco Village Network runs on a simple idea: if you want to build the communities of the future, you have to learn from the ones that have already thrived, past to present. Ashu is a prolific documenter of India’s intentional communities on Instagram and YouTube, and has spent years building relationships with the farms and villages this caravan visits.

#### About Morello

Morello spent 18 months visiting 40+ intentional communities across multiple continents researching alternative land ownership and regenerative living, and writes and hosts content on solarpunk, land trusts, and community experiments. This caravan is Morello’s continuation of that fieldwork, this time in partnership with Edge City, and in a country Morello considers essential to understanding what resourceful, land-based living actually looks like at scale.

“If you’re into solarpunk and living off the land, Indians are some of the most resourceful people, seeing these long-standing communities in action is a must.”

#### About Edge City India

Edge City India is a three-week popup village on the beaches of Mandrem, Goa, running October 11 to November 1. It brings together hundreds of founders, builders, researchers, scientists, and creatives to live and work together.

It’s the Indian edition of a global series of popup villages, and the first time the Edge City community lands in India, one of the world’s largest builder ecosystems.
