Edge City India is what happens when you put a few hundred builders, researchers, and creators in the same village in North Goa for three weeks (Oct 11 - Nov 1). Give them a shared rhythm of deep work, movement, and evening conversations, and what appears is something like a cross between a college campus and a small town, where people arrive with ideas and leave with projects, companies, and collaborators.

Here’s what your time might look like, whether you’re staying for a week or the full three.

### What is a popup village?

**[Popup villages](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/what-is-a-popup-village)** are an entirely new kind of gathering. Unlike a conference, hackathon, or retreat:

-   People live here, rather than just attend.

-   Work happens organically and daily, not just in scheduled bursts.

-   Ideas develop over weeks instead of being rushed into a 20-minute panel.

-   Co-creation replaces passive consumption. Everyone contributes to shaping the experience.

-   Real communities form, built on shared meals, shared space, and three full weeks together.

### Daily life & weekly flow

**7:30 AM – 9:30 AM: Start with movement.** Morning yoga, HIIT, run club on Mandrem’s long flat sand, or a swim in a sea that stays around 28°C. These become daily rituals with friends.

**9:30 AM – 10:00 AM: Breakfast** at Riva Beach Resort, our community hub on Mandrem Beach, or at a café nearby where you’ll recognize the same faces day after day, and conversations build and deepen naturally.

**10:00 AM – 1:00 PM: Morning programming at Riva.** Talks, workshops, and open residency sessions from that week’s themed troack. Kids head to the Edge Tomorrow hub for their own studio hours a few minutes away.

**1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Lunch** with fellow participants at Riva, at the coworking hub, or at a local spot where your ticket gets you discounts.

**2:00 PM – 6:00 PM: Choose your path:**

-   Go heads-down at The Circle, our coworking hub in Ashvem, open 8am to midnight with meeting rooms and fast WiFi.

-   Take a walking meeting with someone you met at that morning’s sessions.

-   On Fridays, this window includes Demo Day where residents show what they’ve been building.

**6:00 PM: Sunset.** Mandrem faces west over the Arabian Sea, so the whole village drifts to the beach around six for volleyball, football, or nothing at all.

**7:00 PM onward: Dinner and evenings.** Dinner with the village, then wind down with a sauna or a slow evening at Riva, or check the calendar, because there’s always something on, an array of salon discussions, sauna sessions, talks, and things that don’t fit any category, some running deep into the night.

**Special days:**

-   **Sundays bring the village together with a** **town hall and a community dinner**, the weekly reset every Edge City village runs.

-   **Weekends are for adventure**, whether that’s kayaking the mangrove tunnels of the Chapora River, beach-hopping north to Arambol or south to Morjim’s turtle-nesting beach, taking the ferry to Tiracol Fort, or making the full-day trip to Dudhsagar Falls in the Western Ghats.

While this daily structure provides rhythm, the week has its own flow, with weekdays leaning toward focused work and smaller group sessions while weekends bring a higher density of programming and fresh energy as day and weekend visitors join the village.

### The two hubs

Village life runs between two anchors, with **[Riva Beach Resort on Mandrem Beach](https://www.instagram.com/rivabeachresort/)** as the gathering place, home to the pools, the spa, direct beach access, and the daily programming, and **[The Circle on Ashvem Beach](https://www.instagram.com/thecirclework/)** as the place where the work happens. You can walk along the beach between the two, and we’ve also negotiated discounted taxi rates between the two, shuttles run at peak times, and plenty of people just rent a scooter.
