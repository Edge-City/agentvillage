We want you to nourish while you flourish at Edge City India, so we’ve set up a weekday lunch plan with two amazing local kitchens.

Below is **how to book**, **what’s on the menu** each week, **what it costs**, and where your wristband gets you a **discount** when you eat out.

### TL;DR

-   **₹3,000 a week** (about $36) for five lunches, Monday to Friday. That’s around $7 a meal.

-   **Delivered to [The Circle](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AGGVgB3Gu8V1bgAd8)** between 1 and 2pm, individually packed so you can eat wherever you’re working.

-   **[Book one plan per week](https://portal.edgecity.live/checkout/edge-india/lunch-tickets)**. All three weeks are open to book!

-   **Order deadlines.** Week 1 closes October 10, week 2 October 16, and week 3 October 21, at 10am IST.

-   **No refunds once a week is booked**, so the kitchens can cook the right amount.

* * *

## Lunch meal plan

Two local kitchens are cooking, and every option is a full lunch with a sweet, individually packed, for the same ₹3,000 a week.

#### Weeks 1 (12 - 16 Oct) and 3 (26 - 30 Oct) | Om Cafe

A home-style Indian thali that changes every day, with dal or curry, two vegetable dishes, rice, rotis, salad, papad, and pickle. Choose **Vegan**, or **Normal**, which adds a chicken or prawn dish on alternating days.

#### Week 2 (19 - 23 Oct) | Hari Cafe

A different bowl every day, like Thai curry, Mediterranean, or peri peri paneer, each with a dessert. Choose **Vegetarian** or **Vegan**.

## How to book

1.  Open the **[lunch booking page](https://portal.edgecity.live/checkout/edge-india/lunch-tickets)**.

2.  Pick the week or weeks you want and your option, Vegan or Normal for the Om Cafe weeks, Vegetarian or Vegan for the Hari Cafe week.

3.  Pay, and you’re set. Your lunch shows up at [The Circle](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AGGVgB3Gu8V1bgAd8) each day from 1pm, individually packaged so you can take it back to your desk, the garden, or wherever you’re working.

## Order deadlines

-   Week 1, closes Saturday 10 October at 10am IST

-   Week 2, closes Friday 16 October at 10am IST

-   Week 3, closes Wednesday 21 October

Each week closes a few days before it starts so the kitchens know how much to cook. If you’re flying in around a deadline, book before you travel.

## What it costs

**₹3,000 a week** (about $36) for five lunches, Monday to Friday, so around $7 a meal, and every option costs the same. You buy a plan for each week you’re here, so a three-week stay is three plans.

## Eating out & partner discounts

Mandrem’s beach shacks, cafes, and family restaurants are a big part of what makes the village feel like home, and a **[growing list](https://edgecity.notion.site/Edge-City-India-2026-Wiki-038d45cdfc5983c7a1fe013fdc77135b)** of them are offering Edge City attendees a discount when you show your wristband.

-   **[Brisa by the Beach](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Brisa+by+the+Beach+Mandrem+Goa)**, 20% off. Continental, beachfront, open 12pm to 12am.

-   **[Jadugari](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Jaadugari+Morjim+Goa)**, 20% off. Gourmet Indian in Morjim, about 10 minutes by car.

-   **[Lush Garden](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Lush+Garden+Arambol+Goa)**, 20% off. Healthy, mostly vegetarian.

-   **[Zorba Vibes](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Zorba+Vibes+Ashvem+Goa)**, 20% off. North Indian, Chinese, seafood and bar food on Ashvem Beach Road, open 9am to 10pm.

-   **[Hari Cafe](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Hari+Cafe+Mandrem+Goa)**, 15% off. Coffee and healthy, fully vegetarian, two minutes from The Circle. Weekdays only, and it doesn’t stack with the lunch plan.

-   **[Om Cafe](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Om+Cafe+Mandrem+Goa)**, 15% off. Healthy Indian.

-   **[Oo! Olivia](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Oo+Olivia+Mandrem+Goa)**, 15% off. International.

-   **[Cuebebar on the Beach](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cuebebar+on+the+Beach+Ashwem+Goa)**, 15% off. BBQ, in The Circle building.

-   **[Wango Wango](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Wango+Waango+Mandrem+Goa)**, 15% off. South and North Indian on the Arambol side of Mandrem, opens for the season in October.

-   **[Ashwe 351](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Ashwe+351+Mandrem+Goa)**, 10% off. Multi cuisine, inside Palma Beach Resort.

These are local businesses whose owners have welcomed us in, so please make a point of stopping by, whether for a coffee at Hari Cafe, dinner at Brisa by the Beach, or anywhere else on the list.
