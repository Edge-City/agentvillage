## What’s the Community Calendar for?

Edge City India runs on what people bring. We set a theme for each week, environments of tomorrow, longevity, and frontier intelligence, and run **[core programming](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/programming-preview-for-edge-city)** around it, with residencies and partner experiments alongside. Everything else on the calendar comes from attendees, and that’s usually where the best moments are.

That’s how a **[typical day](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/a-typical-day-and-week-at-edge-city?r=7jhl3k&utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web)** ends up being a sunrise workout, a workshop on something you’ve never touched, lunch at [The Circle](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3FpJqyehDNNHZou79) with people you met the night before, a walk and talk, a demo, and a late dinner somewhere in Mandrem.

All of it lives on the **[Community Calendar](https://portal.edgecity.live/portal/edge-india/events)**. See what others are bringing, RSVP to what you want to be part of, and add your own.

## Browsing, RSVPing, and Agentic Access

Once you’re logged in you’ll see the full schedule. You can view it as a list, a calendar, or day by venue, and filter by tag, track, or keyword. Click any event to see the details and RSVP to save your place.

Your RSVPs and anything you’re hosting show up under My RSVPs and My Events. Hosts use the numbers to pick rooms and plan food, so only RSVP to what you plan to go to.

If you use an AI agent, you can give it the calendar too. Create an API key in the portal, hand it to Claude Code, Codex, or whatever you use, and your agent can read the schedule, tell you what’s worth your morning, and RSVP on your behalf. It’s also your first step into **[Agent Village V2](https://x.com/JoinEdgeCity/status/2104956750374085113)**, our experiment in what people and their agents can get done together.

## The weekly rhythm

-   **Opening ceremony.** Sunday, October 11, 5 to 9pm at [The Forresta Kinaro](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=15.6499709,73.7164049), where we kick off the three weeks together. **[RSVP on Luma](https://luma.com/fbh5z4kp)**.

-   **Town hall, demo day, and community dinner.** Every Sunday we catch up on what’s changing, watch what people have been building, and eat together.

-   **Community dinners.** Most nights we take over a partner restaurant, and your wristband discount applies.

-   **Locals night.** On Fridays we open the village to our neighbours in Mandrem.

## Hosting your own session

People come to Edge City from every discipline, and the best way to share what you know is to host a session. That could be a workshop on the thing you’ve been building, a sunrise run on Ashvem beach, a dinner for people working on the same problem, a film screening, or a walk and talk with no agenda. If you have a ticket, you can host it.

When you create an event you choose the date, the format, and the place. You can pick one of the shared village venues or add your own location. Events can be public, private for people you invite, or unlisted with a shareable link. Some venues need approval, so post early.

If you lead a residency, use the same calendar for any session you want to open to the whole village.

## A few tips

-   Bookmark the calendar and check it each morning

-   Add anything you RSVP to into your own calendar so it doesn’t slip past you

-   Go to one session a week that has nothing to do with your field

-   If you’re hosting, give it a clear title and a couple of tags so people can find it

-   If the venue you want is taken, try the day by venue view to find one that’s free

The calendar is only as good as what we all put on it, so add what you’re running as early as you can.
