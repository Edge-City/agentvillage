## **Residencies at Edge City India**

Edge City India is a popup village for builders, researchers, creatives, thinkers, and dreamers with bold ideas and a love for frontier fields. For three weeks we’ll gather in Mandrem, North Goa, to prototype new ways of living and working together, surrounded by inspiring peers from around the globe.

The village centers on residencies, temporary living labs built around a shared theme, where groups of roughly 5 to 50 people co-live, collaborate, and experiment together. Some are fast-paced sprints and others are slow and reflective, and many blend both. Past residencies have sparked open-source tools, research breakthroughs, venture funding, and lifelong friendships, and India is our most residency-driven village yet.

### **How to join a residency**

Joining a residency at Edge City India is a simple process:

1️⃣ [Apply to attend Edge City India](https://portal.edgecity.live/portal/edge-india). Tell us a bit about yourself and how you’d like to participate.

2️⃣ Purchase your ticket once your application is accepted. [Prices increase regularly](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/tickets-for-edge-city-india-2026?r=7jhl3k&utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web), so applying early helps you join at a lower rate.

3️⃣ With a valid ticket, you can join a residency. Most run their own short application, so pick your cohort below and apply directly.

4️⃣ Get your visa. Most nationalities can apply online for an e-Tourist Visa through [Naytive](https://naytive.com/edgecityvisa), our recommended visa partner, and applying early gives you room for any delays.

5️⃣ Book your accommodation and flights. Our discounted rates at [Riva Beach Resort](https://forms.fillout.com/t/eGE4xizEwbus) are first-come-first-served, and [Getting to Edge City India](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/getting-to-edge-city-india) covers flights, transport, and everything in between. Then we’ll see you in Goa!

[Start with step one](https://portal.edgecity.live/portal/edge-india)

### **Agent Village 2.0**

Agent Village returns for its second run, a three-week live experiment where every resident gets a persistent personal AI agent. At [Edge Esmeralda](https://www.edgeesmeralda.com/) the agents brokered introductions, informed community treasury decisions, and began drafting a constitution, and V2 puts them properly to work, finding you collaborators, negotiating introductions and meeting times on your behalf, and helping the village make shared decisions, with human approval built in for anything consequential. There’s no separate application, every resident can take part, and everything we learn is published openly.

**[Read what happened at V1 →](https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/we-gave-a-village-personal-ai-agents)**
