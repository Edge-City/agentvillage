## A Day and Week in the Life at Edge City India

Our goal is for everyone to make real progress on their work, build something meaningful, and leave three weeks healthier, happier and more connected. We’ve designed every part of this to help you get there.

### **The high-level flow**

Mornings start with movement, yoga in the Garden Oasis or Kriya on Fridays, often followed by a swim before the heat arrives and breakfast at Riva or a café nearby, where you’ll start recognising the same faces.

Talks run late morning at Riva. They’re short by design and they set the context for the week’s theme, so you can catch three of them and still have your whole afternoon.

Afternoons are for work. [The Circle](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3FpJqyehDNNHZou79) in Ashvem is our coworking hub, open 09:00 to 01:00, with meeting rooms, fast WiFi and the beach a few steps away. Lunch is delivered there on weekdays if you’ve **[booked the meal plan](https://portal.edgecity.live/checkout/edge-india/lunch-tickets)**.

While daily structure provides rhythm, the week has its own flow, with weekdays leaning toward focused work and smaller group sessions while weekends bring a higher density of programming and fresh energy as day and weekend visitors join the village.

## Your Three Weeks in Goa

This is what three weeks at Edge City India offers: the space to go deep on work that matters, surrounded by people who challenge and inspire you. The rhythm of village life, morning movement, focused afternoons at The Circle, dinners that run long, creates a container where good ideas emerge naturally.

You’ll find yourself healthier from daily movement and warm water, more productive from uninterrupted focus time, and more connected from relationships built over shared meals and late conversations.

If you’re shipping a product, conducting research, building a community, or exploring new ideas, Edge City India gives you three weeks to make tangible progress while living well. Come ready to build, ready to explore, and ready to be surprised by what emerges.
