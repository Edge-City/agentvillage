Applications are open. **Prices will be going up regularly between now and October 11**, so the earlier you grab your ticket, the more you save.

[Apply to attend →](https://www.edgecity.live/india26)

## Pricing Principles

This is how we’re approaching pricing for Edge City India 2026:

**Encourage longer stays.** The full three weeks give the most immersive experience. A large part of what we test at each village is different ways of living and working together, and the longer you stay, the more that experiment pays off. The longer you stay, the cheaper the cost per day.

**Local pricing.** Indian citizens receive 50% off the base ticket price. Please indicate you’re a local in your application. ID required at check-in.

**Scholarships.** We want Edge City India to be welcoming to researchers, scientists, young builders, open-source developers, and artists. We offer a limited number of scholarship tickets for those who need financial assistance and can contribute in meaningful ways. Scholarship recipients commit to roughly 10 hours per week of volunteer effort during the event. Please indicate your need for a scholarship in your application.

**Volunteering.** We’re looking for motivated people willing to volunteer in exchange for a discounted ticket. Prices depend on hours volunteered per week. Apply here.

**Welcoming to families.** Edge City India is for all ages. Kids under 3 are free. Kids and teens (4–17) get 40% off the price of an adult ticket, and their ticket includes **Edge Tomorrow**, our kids residency, at no extra cost. Spouses or partners receive 10% off their ticket.

## Residencies

Anyone can host a residency at Edge City India. Residencies are self-organized by community members. If you have an idea for one, **[propose a residency here](https://edgecity.notion.site/ECI26-How-to-Host-a-Residency-at-Edge-City-India-2026-38dd45cdfc5981d08905edefe91c6f05)**.

A residency is a group of 5 to 50 people who co-live and work together around a shared theme, with their own programming inside the wider village.

**Important:** If you’re planning to join through a residency, you’ll still need an Edge City India ticket. Your residency provides focused programming; your ticket unlocks access to the full village; village-wide events, spaces, wellbeing programming, and the broader community.

Some residencies cover their participants’ tickets and accommodation. We recommend buying your ticket before applying to a residency to secure your spot.

Residency announcements are rolling. Ticket holders get early access.

## Patron Tickets

This tier includes all aspects of a standard ticket, with an additional donation to our 501(c)(3). Patron tickets help keep prices accessible for builders, researchers, and young people, and help fund scholarships for those with less financial means.

Patron tickets start at $7,500.

## What’s Included in Your Ticket

All tickets include:

-   Access to all sessions, seminars, events, hackathons, workshops, and beyond

-   Access to our collaborative calendar to host your own events

-   Coworking space in Mandrem, with meeting rooms and fast WiFi

-   Group wellness activities, including daily workouts

-   Community events, including village dinners

-   Access to the Edge City India chat groups

-   Discounts at partner venues in Mandrem and North Goa

_**Please note:** **Accommodation and daily meals are not included in the ticket price.** Once you’re accepted, we’ll share a housing coordination sheet with villas, boutique hotels, and partner options. Community dinners run throughout the three weeks; Goa’s food scene handles the rest. More info on housing coming soon._

* * *

## How Ticket Proceeds Are Spent

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our intention is for ticket revenue to cover event expenses. Creating a memorable experience involves real costs, and it’s important that this event is self-sustaining.

Major costs include:

-   Infrastructure and logistics

-   Leasing our coworking anchor

-   Building out event and community spaces

-   Community events, including village dinners

-   Wellbeing activities and facilities

-   Local production support

-   Legal fees, visa support, and insurance

-   Scholarships for those with less financial means

-   Core team’s time (planning started months in advance)
