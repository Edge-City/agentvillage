**[Edge City](https://www.edgecity.live/)** has always been family friendly, and our **[popup villages](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/what-is-a-popup-village)** are built for every generation at once.

At **[Edge City India](https://www.edgecity.live/india26)**, running October 11 to November 1 in Mandrem, Goa, kids and teens are full citizens of the village. There’s a dedicated space for families, an intergenerational creative hub where kids lead their own creating and learning, and everyone in the village is encouraged to build alongside them.

Every village turns out a little different given new locations and collaborations, we’re looking forward to seeing what we co-create together in Goa.

Here is everything your family needs to know to join us.

### TL;DR

-   **Edge Tomorrow is a creative environment for families**, running alongside the main village for one week or all three.

-   **Kids build real projects with real makers**, supported by the founders, artists, builders, and technologists around them, with plenty of downtime built in.

-   **Kids under 3 are covered too**, with free caregiver tickets, shared family spaces, and trusted local nannies we can connect you with.

-   **Parents are part of the family experience.** This is an intergenerational village, and families shape it together.

-   **The Family Village Residency** clusters family accommodation within walking distance of the hub, so kids find each other without a schedule and parents find each other too.

-   **Kids and teens get 40% off the adult [ticket](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/tickets-for-edge-city-india-2026?r=7jhl3k&utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web)**, whether that’s the full three weeks, one week, or a day pass. Kids under 3 are free.

-   **[Apply now](https://portal.edgecity.live/portal/edge-india)**, add a kids ticket at checkout, and you’re all set.

### What to expect

We’re co-creating this with the families who come, drawing on what we’ve learned about kids-led creativity at past villages and will have extra caregivers on the grond. Here’s roughly how it works.

**The family hub.** A dedicated space in the village where kids lead their own creating and learning. A teacher and two nannies are on hand every day, and it’s the home base families come back to between everything else.

**A typical day**

-   **Morning at the family hub.** The kids decide what gets made, whether that’s a cardboard city, a small business, a film, or a giant installation.

-   **Afternoon with the village.** Your kid joins you at a talk or workshop, or heads to the beach with the other families.

-   **Evening together.** Community dinners are for everyone, and the calendar leans toward things the whole family can do, like swims, all-ages movement sessions, and film nights.

**Everyone’s invited to take part.** Attendees in the village have the opportunity to sign up to run an activity for the kids through the community calendar, so if you’d like to share a skill, a half-finished project, or a story, the kids will be an amazing audience.

**If you need childcare.** We have a list of trusted local nannies you can hire, and many families set up a babysitting club between themselves. That’s a lot easier when you’re living next door to each other, which is where the Family Village comes in.

### The Family Village

The Family Village is a cluster of accommodation within walking distance of the hub and the main village spaces, so kids find each other without a schedule, parents become neighbours rather than acquaintances who met at a session, and the daily puzzles of coverage, timing, and getting places get solved together instead of separately.

Alongside it there are lightly facilitated points of connection and reflection on the Edge City experience, and on raising and educating children for the times we’re in. Edge City moves fast, and for families there’s an added layer, integrating everything you’re each encountering in ways that connect you rather than spread you thin. Finding a rhythm that works is each family’s own to discover, but you don’t have to do it alone or from scratch.

The residency is led by Edge Esmeralda parents Keri and Jonathan Shinn, who have worldschooled their four kids for a decade. Much of what the residency becomes will be co-created by the families in it.

The cluster and the cohorts are small, so you apply rather than sign up, and housing is reserved as families commit.

### What it costs

-   **Kids and teens (4 to 17)** get 40% off the adult ticket, whether that’s the full three weeks, one week, or a day pass. The family hub is included.

-   **Kids under 3** are free.

-   **Nannies, caregivers, and elders (65+)** are free too, so bring your whole crew.

-   **Indian citizens** get 50% off all tickets.

-   **Large families** can ask about group discounts, and there are volunteer roles across the village that help offset costs.

Prices go up as the event approaches, so the earlier you apply, the better your price.

Full details live in the **[Edge City India ticket guide](https://edgecityindia2026.substack.com/p/tickets-for-edge-city-india-2026)**.

### FAQ

**How do I sign up?** Apply to Edge City India, include your children in the application, and add their tickets at checkout once you’re accepted. If you have any questions along the way, email **[info@edgecity.live](mailto:info@edgecity.live)**.

**Is this drop-off childcare?** It isn’t, and that’s by design. Kids belong in the village, parents are part of the family experience, and families coordinate coverage together. If you do need dedicated care during the day, we can connect you with trusted local nannies.

**What about school?** Three weeks in the village is learning by doing. Kids scope, build, and share real things with a real community, alongside peers from around the world and worldschooling families who’ve been doing this for years.

**Will my kid know anyone?** Many kids arrive without knowing anyone, and most leave with friends from all over the world. The family hub and the Family Village housing put kids in each other’s way every day, so friendships form fast and without a plan.

**Where in the village is the family hub?** Within an easy, safe walk of the main Edge City spaces and the family housing cluster.

**My child is 3 or under. What’s available?** Caregivers and nannies attend free, and we can connect you with trusted local childcare in Goa through our local partners. The Family Village is also a lovely community for parents of little ones, so you’ll have company and support.

**Can my child bring friends?** Of course. Your child is welcome to invite a friend their age to apply and join them.

**I’m an adult at Edge without kids and I want to be involved.** We’d love that. Once the community calendar opens, sign up to run an activity for the kids, whether you’re a parent, an auntie, an uncle, or someone with a skill worth passing on.

**I have more questions.** Email **[info@edgecity.live](mailto:info@edgecity.live)**.

### Why we’re building this

Most kids grow up in an age-segregated world, moving between rooms full of people exactly their own age, and most parents feel the waste in that when their kids could be connecting with fascinating adults, building real things, and getting a fuller sense of who they are in connection to something larger. Edge City is a village where the generations live, build, and learn together, and we think both the kids and the adults come out better for it.
