## Step 2: Fly to Goa

Goa has two airports.

-   **Manohar International Airport (GOX)**, also called Mopa, is your destination. It sits in North Goa, roughly 40 to 60 minutes from Mandrem.

-   **Dabolim Airport (GOI)** also works, but it’s in South Goa and roughly double the drive.

Most people either fly into GOX directly through Gulf hubs like Doha and Sharjah, or connect through Delhi, Mumbai, or Bangalore and take a short domestic flight to Goa. Check the routes from your home airport and book whichever works best for you.

🃏 **Wild card: take the caravan instead.** The Road to Edge travels overland from Bangalore to Goa in the ten days before the village opens, moving through South India’s farms and regenerative communities and arriving at Edge City exactly as week one begins. ## Step 3: From the airport to Mandrem

Once you land, you have four ways to reach the village.

-   **Carpool with your fellow attendees** to the village.

-   **[Airport taxis](https://www.miagoaairport.com/taxi-service)** run straight to [Riva Beach Resort](https://forms.fillout.com/t/eGE4xizEwbus), where check-in happens, for around $5.

-   **Pre-arranged pickups** can be booked with your accommodation when you reserve your room.

-   **[GoaMiles](https://www.goamiles.com/)** is Goa’s ride app, running instead of Uber and Ola, so download it before you land if you prefer booking on your phone, but this is less reliable than prepaid taxis

Whichever you choose, save your accommodation’s location pin offline before you fly, since drivers often navigate by pin better than by street name. Wifi is also available in the airport, just in case!

## Before you fly

-   **Set up an eSIM before you leave home** so you land with working data. Physical SIMs are available at the airport but take longer to set up.

-   **Bring a card and some cash.** Cards work at hotels and bigger shops, but India’s everyday payment system needs an Indian bank account, so carry some rupees for cafés and small vendors (airport ATMs cover you on arrival). An RFID-blocking sleeve for your cards is a cheap bit of peace of mind in busy airports and markets anywhere in the world. UPI partnership discussions for Indian payment systems are also underway, which is particularly useful if you plan to stay in India after Edge City.

-   **Pack light and breathable.** October in Goa is warm, so bring light clothes, swimwear, a layer for air-conditioned rooms, sunscreen, and mosquito repellent.

-   **Bring a universal adapter.** India runs Type C and D plugs at 230V.

-   **Consider travel insurance** for a three-week trip abroad.

## Getting around Edge City India

The village is built for walking via beach or transport via road. **[The Circle](https://www.thecircle.work/goa),** **[Riva Beach Resort](https://forms.fillout.com/t/eGE4xizEwbus)**, and the beach itself are all close together in Mandrem, so most days transport will be limited.

For everything further out, you have options.

-   **[Scooter rentals](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Bike+scooter+rental+Goa/@15.2869475,73.8994128,7614m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!5m1!1e2?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDgyNC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)** are everywhere in Goa and cost a few dollars a day, and riding one is how much of the coast gets around. Bring a valid driving license if you plan to ride, and wear a helmet.

-   **[GoaMiles](https://www.goamiles.com/)** and **[local taxis](https://www.google.com/maps/search/taxi+service+near+Goa,+India/@15.4597109,73.7077672,60858m/data=!3m1!1e3!5m1!1e2?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDgyNC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)** cover the distance otherwise, and the community Telegram is the place to find people to share rides with.
