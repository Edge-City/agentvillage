This October, six Inflection Fellows join Edge City India for three weeks in Mandrem, North Goa (October 11 to November 1). The fellowship, run with our partner **[Long Journey](https://www.longjourney.vc/)**, gives each of them a full ride: housing, travel, and ticket covered, plus access to the hundreds of builders, researchers, and artists living in the village.

Thanks for reading Edge City India 2026! Subscribe for free to receive new posts and support my work.

This is the second Inflection cohort of 2026, after [Edge Esmeralda](https://edgeesmeralda2026.substack.com/p/recap-the-inflection-fellowship-at?r=7jhl3k&utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web) in California, and the first in India. We chose these six from a very competitive pool for the same reasons we always do: high agency, a magically weird project, and something real to give the people around them.

Meet the fellows.

## Linda Vinod (Mumbai)

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FTbB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa7f351f0-d901-4675-b6f5-1af93a5308c5_1024x1024.png)

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FTbB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa7f351f0-d901-4675-b6f5-1af93a5308c5_1024x1024.png)

Linda thinks it’s strange that a person’s data is the internet’s most valuable product, owned and monetized by everyone except the person who generated it. She’s building a way for people to decide where their data flows, get compensated when it moves, and pull access from anyone they’ve stopped trusting. At Edge, she will test that thesis by building and launching the product to a beachhead market.

When she’s not chasing this particular curiosity, Linda leads product and community for the membership app of world lacrosse champion Charlotte North. Before that, she ran writing programs for communities like The Network State and Ethereum Foundation and once built an in-person open-mic community of 10k+ Gen Z artists.

[lindavinod.com](https://www.lindavinod.com/)

## Luna Mustfa (London)

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pnju!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F62c4460d-076b-41a7-a78c-6a3b5dfaa445_1024x1024.png)

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pnju!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F62c4460d-076b-41a7-a78c-6a3b5dfaa445_1024x1024.png)

Luna works at the intersection of AI and politics. She was Director of Communications to Kanishka Narayan, the UK’s first Cabinet Minister for AI, where she worked on communicating all things AI to the public from the heart of government. From London, she is now moving to San Francisco to focus on strategic comms for AI safety organisations. She also blogs about art and loves hiking.

## Adrian Cipriani (Munich)

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9o8W!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc1c30abe-2702-42a4-bd54-6cc7a0e338db_1024x1024.png)

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9o8W!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc1c30abe-2702-42a4-bd54-6cc7a0e338db_1024x1024.png)

Adrian is driven to make interactions with computers and AI more empowering and natural for humans. As machines become superhuman in some aspects, he thinks we need to intentionally redesign our relationship with them, to avoid the looming insignificance of humans in creative and scientific pursuits. He recently left his undergrad in engineering because this feels more important.

He co-hosts retreats with his organisation Ursprung, a running experiment in building creative interdisciplinary environments among builders and thinkers from all walks of life.

[cipriani.studio](https://cipriani.studio/) | [@ostreion](https://x.com/ostreion)

## Ajay Pavan (Bengaluru)

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o9te!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F562c174b-41e1-411b-9fe4-e2a1ba4cf6a7_1024x1024.png)

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o9te!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F562c174b-41e1-411b-9fe4-e2a1ba4cf6a7_1024x1024.png)

At Edge, Ajay is building a tool that turns raw genotype data into a mythology instead of a plain medical report. It’ll be the genomic equivalent of what Spotify Wrapped did for music, treating your mutations as the origin story of a creature only you could be, traced to real variants in your genome.

Ajay was the Founding R&D Engineer at BioCompute Inc., a startup focused on storing digital data in DNA, where he led the proof of concept experiments and is named inventor on a filed US patent. Since then, he’s worked at the intersection of biology and machine learning, building NGS pipelines, drug design workflows, EEG and biosignal models, and genomic tools for biotech clients around the world.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ajay-pavan-8a80761bb/)
